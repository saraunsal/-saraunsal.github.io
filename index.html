About Me

EnglishArabicFrench

My background sits between two worlds. My associate degree taught me how pharmacies and healthcare organizations run: medicines, patients, records and the administration behind them. My bachelor's degree in Business Information Technology taught me the systems and data that businesses depend on.

In my work with a car services company, I deal directly with customers and business clients every day. That experience taught me that good analysis starts with the people behind the numbers. I rebuilt six months of scattered weekly records into one clean dataset, then turned it into a dashboard and a written analysis.

I am currently working on a series of healthcare analytics projects using Medicare, FDA and clinical trial data.
